Project One • PDP Strategy

Project Two • Video Production Strategy

Template-Driven Video Ecosystems at Scale

Designing an affordable, regulatory-compliant video matrix across 200+ unique SKUs without inflating operational design overhead.

The Challenge With over 200 products, each with its own set of instructions, creating a unique “how to use” video for every item was expensive. Retailers needed the content, customers demanded it, but producing 200 custom videos? We had to find a smarter way to deliver what everyone needed, without blowing the budget.
The Insight While individual EPA-regulated instruction copies vary widely, physical packaging geometries remain completely uniform.
The Execution Engineered a modular layout methodology utilizing live-action hand models paired with 3D product renders. Enabled variable instruction blocks to swap dynamically into standard master video timelines.
Project Three • UX & Web Transformation

Reimagining .Com for the Modern Gardener

Transforming BioAdvanced.com from a standard transactional catalog layout into an educational, solution-first digital consumer hub.

The Challenge The original site struggled to balance product discovery with education. Users were overwhelmed by dense content and unclear navigation, making it harder to find what they needed.
The Insight By aligning UX research, SEO insights, and content strategy, we uncovered a key opportunity: users weren’t just browsing, they were looking for solutions. They needed a site that could guide them quickly from question to product to purchase.
The Execution We redesigned the site to simplify product discovery, integrate helpful content, and support retail conversion via PriceSpider. The result is a platform that balances brand storytelling with practical lawn and garden solutions, positioning BioAdvanced as both a trusted authority and a sales driver.
